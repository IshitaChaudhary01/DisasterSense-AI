SimCLR Contrastive Self-Supervised Pretraining Framework

Our pipeline trains an encoder on unlabelled 2-channel Sentinel-1 SAR imagery using contrastive multiview learning. Augmented views of the same geographic chip are pushed together in hyperspherical embedding space while views of distinct chips are pushed apart.
